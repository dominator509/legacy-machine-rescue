# Product: Legacy Machine Rescue

For machine shops whose CNC machines depend on an old Windows 98 or DOS PC. Input: a disk image. Output: an inspected, emulator-tested recovery runbook.

## Honesty rules (never break these)
- An emulator boot proves the disk contents start. It never proves the real motion card, hardware key (dongle), serial/DNC link or CNC controller works. Every report and demo must say so.
- Every claim in a report is labelled MEASURED (read from the image), EMULATOR (observed in the emulator) or NOT PROVEN.
- No stubbed or faked results. Demos use real emulator frames and real guest screen text.
- Test images are FreeDOS (GPL) or clearly labelled synthetic. No copyrighted OS images, no real shop data.

## Audience and tone
Shop owners and technicians. Plain, professional wording. Short sentences. Instructions section in the README.
